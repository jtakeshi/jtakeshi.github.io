Excellence in Cryptography and Privacy Award
==
The Excellence in Cryptography and Privacy Award is awarded to ODU undergraduate or non-doctoral graduate 
students who demonstrate outstanding achievement in cryptography, privacy, or closely related areas. 
The award is competitive, with one award being granted per semester. 
Any or all of strong performance in courses, research excellence, or service to academic units, the University, or any other 
institution may be criteria for the award.
