Gave a talk at the University of Shizuoka (静岡県立大学)! The talk was titled "Privacy-Enhancing Technologies and their Business Implications (プライバシー強化技術とビジネスへの影響)". A flyer can be found [here](./files/shizuken-talk-2026.pdf).

The talk was given twice, once to a 140-student introductory course, and again to a senior-level seminar.

I wrote some custom code in LaTeX to allow conditional compilation of the talk slides to either English or Japanese, while also adding English note slides.
